About the Team

Andrea Del Popolo
Freediver & Spearfisher | Greece

I am Andrea Del Popolo, a freediver and spearfisher based in Greece. I love exploring the sea, spending time underwater, and sharing my dives through photos and videos. I'm part of the Ariel Spearfishing Team, and I enjoy documenting our trips, catches, and the beauty of the Mediterranean.

When I'm not diving, I'm usually training, traveling with friends, or working on new projects that combine my passion for the ocean with creativity and storytelling.
